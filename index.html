How it works

i

Deposit

Gift USDT to a friend or customer to buy KAVI for them.

ii

The target

Your chosen plan sets the unlock price. Nothing can be claimed until KAVI trades at or above it.

iii

Ten claims

Once unlocked, the beneficiary claims a tenth of the target each month, sold to USDT at market.

iv

Everyone earns

Each claim pays 10% to you as the funder and 10% to the platform. The beneficiary keeps 80%.

Understand this before funding

The position stays locked until its price target is met, and if the market never reaches it, nothing can ever be claimed. Payouts are produced by selling the locked KAVI at market, so the amount received depends on the price at each claim. Every claim also sells KAVI into the pool. So fund a position only if you believe KAVI can reach the multiple you choose.
